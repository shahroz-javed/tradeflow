these are the ONLY setups i trade — nothing else

SETUP 1: Trend Continuation Pullback (highest probability)
    timeframe: H1 entry, H4 bias
    condition: clear uptrend or downtrend on H4
    zone: price pulls back to trend continuation S/R zone
    entry: hammer (long) or shooting star (short) at zone
    confirmation: next candle closes in trade direction
    fib: candle body within 38.2-61.8 retracement
    SL: beyond zone (below demand / above supply)
    TP: previous high (long) or previous low (short)
    R:R: minimum 1:2

SETUP 2: Supply/Demand Zone Reaction (fresh zones only)
    timeframe: H1 entry, H4 zone
    condition: fresh zone (first or second touch)
    zone: RBR/DBR (demand) or DBD/RBD (supply)
    entry: confirmation candle at zone edge
    SL: beyond zone bottom/top
    TP: next opposing zone
    R:R: minimum 1:2

DO NOT TRADE:
    choppy/ranging market with no clear structure
    during high-impact news
    when no clear zone is visible
    when R:R is less than 1:2
    counter-trend without reversal confirmation
