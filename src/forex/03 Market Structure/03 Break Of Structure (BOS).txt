Analogy: a runner setting a new personal record


If you're in an uptrend and price pushes above the previous Higher High, that's a BOS — it confirms "the trend is still intact, new record set." It's confirmation, not a warning. 


In a downtrend, BOS means price breaks below the previous Lower Low — same idea, just downward.




                Direction of break                      What it tells you
BOSSame         direction as current trend              Trend  continuing — confidence
CHoCH           Opposite direction of current trend     Trend possibly ending — caution
