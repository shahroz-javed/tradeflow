Analogy: zooming out on Google Maps. If you only look at your street (1-minute chart), you might think you're going the "wrong way" — but zoom out to the city level (4-hour or daily chart) and you see you're actually on the right highway heading the correct direction overall.



Why this matters: a CHoCH on the 5-minute chart might just be normal noise inside a much bigger uptrend on the 4-hour chart. 
Professional approach: check the higher timeframe first to know the overall trend, then drop to a lower timeframe to time your actual entry within that bigger picture. Trading against the higher timeframe trend is one of the most common beginner mistakes.
