Plan before trade: [pre-trade]
Define entry and exit before entering
Know when NOT to trade
Set max risk and profit target upfront

Control reaction: [mindset]
Market is uncontrollable — accept it
Plan response if trade goes well
Plan response if trade goes badly


Position sizing: [risk]
Always run risk analysis first
No excitement, no fear — it's just a trade
Size based on rules, not feelings



Accept losses: [discipline]
Losses are part of trading
Cut losses early — never let them grow

When in profit: [patience]
Don't close out of fear — wait for the move
Let winners run, book big profits



Wrong thinking: [avoid]
No strategy changes after a few losses
No revenge/emotional trading
No constant system-switching




Long-term goal: [focus]
Think in 1,000 trades, not 1
Follow rules in every single trade
Keep the process simple


Never lose your capital:
Capital is what keeps you in the game
Opportunities always come back — you must be there
