An order is simply an instruction.

You tell the broker:
    Buy or sell under certain conditions.


There are two categories.
    Instant orders [Market Order]
        Execute immediately.
            Market Buy
            Market Sell
        Use when:
            price is already at your zone
            you want in immediately
            confirmation candle just closed



    Pending orders
        Limit Order:
            Enter at THIS price or better
            Execute only if price reaches a specified level.
            Examples:
                Buy Limit
                Sell Limit

            Use when:
            price hasn't reached your zone yet
            you know exactly where you want in
            you can't watch the screen

        Stop Order:
        "Enter ONLY IF price breaks this level"
        Opposite logic from limit — triggers on breakout

        Buy Stop: place ABOVE current price
            triggers when price breaks up through your level

        Sell Stop: place BELOW current price
            triggers when price breaks down through your level

        Use when:
            trading breakouts
            waiting for price to break structure (BOS)
            confirmation that momentum is in your direction
