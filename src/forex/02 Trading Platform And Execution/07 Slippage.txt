What you wanted:  entry at 1.0800
What you got:     entry at 1.0803

Difference = 3 pips slippage

Why it happens:
  market moves fast between your click and fill
  low liquidity at that exact price
  broker execution speed

How to minimize:
  trade during high liquidity sessions (London, NY)
  use limit orders instead of market orders
  avoid entries right at news releases
