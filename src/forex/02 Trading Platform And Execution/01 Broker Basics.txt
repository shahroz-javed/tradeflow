A broker is the company that connects you to the forex market.
    You → Broker → Liquidity Providers/Banks → Market
    Think of a broker like an internet service provider.



A broker provides:
    Trading platform (e.g., MT5)
    Price quotes
    Order execution
    Leverage
    Account management
    Deposits & withdrawals

Liquidity Providers
    Your broker usually gets prices from:
        Banks
        Financial institutions
        ECNs
        Other liquidity providers
    The broker aggregates these prices and shows you the best available bid and ask.


Types of brokers
    Market Maker (Dealing Desk)
        The broker may become the counterparty to your trade.

        Pros:
            Fast execution
            Small accounts accepted


        Cons:
            Potential conflict of interest (though many regulated brokers manage this fairly)

    ECN/STP
        Your order is routed toward liquidity providers.
        Pros:
            Usually tighter spreads
            Greater transparency

        Cons:
            Commission may apply

    Regulated vs Unregulated
        Always prefer regulated brokers.



Checklist before opening an account
    ✔ Regulation
    ✔ Low spreads
    ✔ Fast execution
    ✔ Good withdrawal reputation
    ✔ Strong customer support
