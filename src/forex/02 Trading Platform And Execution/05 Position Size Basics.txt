Position size answers:
    How large should my trade be?



If your stop loss is 20 pips away, you calculate a lot size that equals a $2 loss if the stop is hit.
This is why position size depends on your stop loss, not the other way around.


The sequence is:
    Find your trade setup.
    Decide where the stop loss belongs based on the chart.
    Decide your acceptable risk (e.g., 1–2%).
    Calculate the lot size.
