This connects rules 1 and 2 together — it answers "how many units/shares/lots do I actually buy?" The formula: Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ Stop-Loss Distance. If you're risking $20 and your stop-loss is $2 away from entry, you buy 10 units. This way, position size is always calculated from your risk tolerance — never guessed or sized based on "gut feeling" or how confident you feel.
