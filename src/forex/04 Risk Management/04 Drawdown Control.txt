Set hard limits: e.g., stop trading for the day after losing 4% of your account, stop for the week after 8%. The math behind why this is critical is brutal — recovering from a drawdown requires a disproportionately larger gain:
Drawdown    Gain needed to recover
10%         11%
20%         25%
50%         100%
80%         400%


This is why protecting capital isn't just a "nice idea" — it's mathematically the most important thing you do.
