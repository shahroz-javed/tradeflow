RISK OF LOSING MONEY

Basic truth:
    • Every trade can lose
    • No guaranteed setup
    • Losing is normal
    • Unplanned losses are dangerous

Only risk:
    • Money you can afford to lose
    • Never rent, bills, emergency fund, borrowed money

Drawdown math:
    • -10% = +11% recovery
    • -20% = +25%
    • -50% = +100%
    • -80% = +400%

Account killers:
    • No stop loss
    • Moving stop loss
    • Overleveraging
    • Oversized positions
    • Revenge trading
    • Overtrading

Psychological risks:
    • Fear
    • Greed
    • Frustration
    • Overconfidence

Risk management:
    • Risk is unavoidable
    • Risk is manageable
    • Control loss size, not outcomes

Position sizing:
    1. Risk amount
    2. Stop loss
    3. Lot size

Before entry ask:
    • What is my risk?
    • Where is my stop?
    • Can I survive 10 losses in a row?

Risk-reward:
    • Higher RR improves expectancy
    • RR alone is not an edge

Journal:
    • Setup
    • Entry
    • SL
    • TP
    • Reason
    • Outcome

Core principle:
    Protect capital first.
    Survival comes before growth.
