REALITY OF FOREX

Market facts:
    • Largest financial market
    • Highly liquid
    • 24/5 market
    • High leverage available

Participants:
    • Banks
    • Hedge funds
    • Institutions
    • Algorithms
    • Retail traders

Retail reality:
    • Most traders lose money
    • Failure usually comes from:
        - poor risk management
        - overleveraging
        - emotional trading
        - inconsistency
        - lack of process

Edge + Risk:
    • Edge creates profits
    • Risk management protects capital
    • Need both

Why traders struggle:
    • Undercapitalized
    • Impatient
    • Strategy hopping
    • Information overload
    • No journal/review

Leverage:
    • Reduces margin requirement
    • Does NOT increase edge
    • Risk should come from risk %, not leverage

Brokers:
    • Prefer regulated brokers
    • Check spreads, commissions, execution

Reality:
    • Forex is legitimate
    • Forex attracts scams
    • Success requires:
        capital preservation
        edge
        discipline
        patience
        consistency

Core principle:
    Most traders fail because they ignore fundamentals and chase excitement.
