EXPECTATIONS

Trading is NOT:
    • Get-rich-quick
    • Guaranteed income
    • A skill mastered in weeks/months
    • Gambling if rules exist (becomes gambling without rules)

Trading IS:
    • A skill like coding, sports, music
    • Probability-based decision making
    • A long-term process requiring screen time and experience

Typical progression:
    • 0-6m: learning, mistakes, losses
    • 6-12m: pattern recognition, inconsistency
    • 1-3y: developing edge, discipline
    • 3y+: consistency becomes possible

Realistic returns:
    • 10-20% yearly is considered excellent by many professionals
    • Consistent daily/weekly high returns = red flag

Expect:
    • Losing trades
    • Emotional challenges
    • Slow growth
    • Strategy refinement

Mindset:
    • Process > Outcome
    • Skill > Prediction
    • Survival > Profit
    • Judge execution, not one trade

Probability:
    • Good trade can lose
    • Bad trade can win
    • Think in series of trades

Core principle:
    Execute a repeatable edge, manage risk, let probabilities play out.
