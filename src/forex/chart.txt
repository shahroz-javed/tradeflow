What to actually look at when watching charts
1. Trend
2. Support & Resistance
3. Candle behavior
4. Volume

You're training your eyes to notice patterns that repeat. Things like:
Price goes up strong → then pulls back a little → then goes up again
Price hits a certain level → bounces back every time
Big candle appears → next candles slow down
After 30 days of watching, these patterns start to feel familiar. That's "absorbing the market."

questions to ask your self:
What direction is price moving?
Is it moving with strength or weakness?
Where is price likely to go next?
Where is price right now?
What did price do at this level before?
Is this an uptrend, downtrend, or sideways?
