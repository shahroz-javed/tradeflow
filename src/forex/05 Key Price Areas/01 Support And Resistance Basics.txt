Support: 
    A zone where buying pressure (demand) exceeds selling pressure
    Price tends to stop falling and bounce upward

Resistance: 
    A zone where selling pressure (supply) exceeds demand
    Price tends to stop rising and move downward

Zone:
    Zone width rule of thumb:
    On most pairs: 5–20 pips wide, not a single price
    Mark the zone from: wick tip to candle body close
        (the body shows where price "accepted" — more important)
        (the wick shows where price "rejected" — the extreme)
    Don't enter at the wick tip — enter near the body close


support and resistance Role reversal:
    Broken resistance → becomes support
    Broken support → becomes resistance


🔹 2 Types of Support
    1. Trend Continuation Support
    Happens in an uptrend
    Previous resistance becomes support
    This is the latest broken resistance level

    👉 Used for buying pullbacks

    2. Counter-Trend Support
    Strong zone where price may reverse from downtrend to uptrend
    Not dependent on recent breakout
    Usually:
    Multiple rejections
    Strong historical level

👉 Used for reversal trades (higher risk)

🔹 2 Types of Resistance
    1. Trend Continuation Resistance
    Happens in a downtrend
    Previous support becomes resistance
    This is the latest broken support level

    👉 Used for selling pullbacks

    2. Counter-Trend Resistance
        Strong zone where price may reverse from uptrend to downtrend
        Not based on recent break
        Usually:
        Strong rejection
        Historical level

    👉 Used for reversal trades


how to draw Support And Resistance zones?



mistakes trader make:
    not able to enter consistantly inside support resistance zone
    support and resistance is line → actually zone ✔️ Correct
    too many zones ✔️ Very important mistake
    ❗ Ignoring higher timeframe levels (very common beginner error)
    ❗ Entering blindly without confirmation


3-step support and  resistance process
    identify trend
    identify trend continuation & counter trend level
    strong level? (caused a major move/tested multiple times)
        A level is strong when:
        tested 2+ times without breaking        ← more tests = more valid
        caused a sharp/impulsive move away      ← shows real buying/selling
        visible on higher timeframe (H4, Daily) ← HTF levels > LTF levels
        round number confluence (1.0800, 160.00 etc) ← psychological levels
        aligns with previous structure (old high/low)
