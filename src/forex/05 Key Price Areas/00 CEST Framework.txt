CEST Framework [A Trader personal not global]:
    Conditions → confluence (trend + S/R Zone + fresh supply/demand zone on HTF + price action + indicator)
    Entry → confirmation (candlestick / pattern)
    Stop Loss → beyond structure (not random)
    Target → next liquidity / S&R level

bullish entry:
    1. Uptrend on higher timeframe (H4/Daily)
    2. Price pulls back to S/R zone
    3. Zone aligns with 38.2–61.8 fabonachi retracement
    4. Hammer candle forms at zone
    5. Next candle closes bullish (confirmation) [green candle]
    6. Enter on confirmation candle close
    7. SL below the hammer wick (beyond structure)
    8. TP at next resistance / liquidity level

use fabonachi for identify hammer candle [entire body should above 38.20 fabonaccie retracement]


Bearish entry full checklist:
  1. Downtrend on higher timeframe (H4/Daily)
  2. Price pulls back to resistance zone
  3. Zone aligns with 38.2–61.8 fib retracement
  4. Shooting star forms at zone
  5. Next candle closes bearish (confirmation)
  6. Enter on confirmation candle close
  7. SL above shooting star wick
  8. TP at next support / liquidity levels

use fabonachi for identify shooting star candle [entire body should under 38.20 fabonaccie retracement]
