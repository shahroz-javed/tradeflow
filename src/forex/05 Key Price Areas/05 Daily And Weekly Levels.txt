Think of it like a city map. Weekly levels are the main highways. Daily levels are the streets. You need to know where the highways are before navigating the streets.


Weekly High/Low:
    highest and lowest price reached this week
    major institutions watch these levels
    price respecting or breaking these = big signal

Daily High/Low:
    highest and lowest price reached today/yesterday
    yesterday's high and low are most important
    price breaking yesterday's high = bullish momentum
    price breaking yesterday's low = bearish momentum

Previous Close:
    where price closed last day/week
    acts as a reference point for bias



Why these levels matter
    Banks and institutions plan trades on Daily/Weekly charts
    They place orders at these obvious levels
    When price reaches weekly high → big sellers likely waiting
    When price reaches weekly low → big buyers likely waiting
    These are NOT random — they are the most watched
    levels in the entire market
