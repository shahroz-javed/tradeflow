when to exit a trade

PLANNED EXIT (good):
    price reached TP level → close trade
    price reached SL level → accept loss, move on

EARLY EXIT — only valid reasons:
    major unexpected news event
    price action completely invalidates the setup
    market structure changes against trade on H4+

NEVER EXIT BECAUSE:
    trade is in small profit and you feel scared
    you feel like "this is enough"
    trade has been open too long (time is not a reason)
    someone else says market is going other way

PARTIAL PROFIT (optional, once consistent):
    close 50% at 1:1 R:R
    move SL to breakeven
    let remaining 50% run to full TP

AFTER EXIT:
    record trade in journal immediately
    screenshot the chart
    note: what worked, what didn't, emotions felt
