Bid — the price you SELL at
Ask — the price you BUY at

Simple analogy — money exchange shop
    You go to exchange shop with USD, want PKR
    USD / PKR
    ↑      ↑
    BASE   QUOTE

    Shop board shows:
    BUY  (they buy your USD):  PKR 277
    SELL (they sell you USD):  PKR 280

    They buy LOW, sell HIGH
    The difference is their profit [called spread]
