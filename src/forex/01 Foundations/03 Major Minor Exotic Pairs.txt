1. Major Pairs
    These contain USD and are the most traded pairs in the world.
    EUR/USD
    GBP/USD
    USD/JPY

    Characteristics:
        Highest liquidity
        Lowest spreads
        Easier to enter/exit trades
        Best for beginners

2. Minor Pairs (Cross Pairs)
    These do not contain USD but are made from major currencies.
    GBP/JPY
    EUR/JPY
    EUR/GBP

    Characteristics:
        Good liquidity
        Slightly larger spreads than majors
        Often move differently from USD pairs

3. Exotic Pairs
    One major currency paired with a currency from an emerging economy.

    USD/TRY (Turkish Lira)
    USD/MXN (Mexican Peso)
    USD/ZAR (South African Rand)

    Characteristics:
        Wide spreads
        Lower liquidity
        More volatile
        Can have large overnight swap charges
