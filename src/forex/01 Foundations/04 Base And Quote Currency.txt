BASE  = the currency you're BUYING or SELLING (always 1 unit)
QUOTE = the currency you're PAYING WITH (the price)
        It is the currency used to value the base
        It tells you the price of 1 base currency

        Example price of 1 USD:
            BUY  (they buy your USD):  PKR 277
            SELL (they sell you USD):  PKR 280


EUR/USD
    EUR = Base currency
    USD = Quote currency


    
You are always trading the BASE currency, and the QUOTE currency is just the pricing unit.


Simple example
    EUR/USD = 1.1000

    This means:
    1 EUR = 1.10 USD

    Now:
        If you BUY EUR/USD:
        You are buying EUR
        And selling USD

        If you SELL EUR/USD:
        You are selling EUR
        And buying USD
