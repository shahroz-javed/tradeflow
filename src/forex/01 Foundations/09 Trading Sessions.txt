📌 Pakistan Time (PKT) Trading Reality

US news mostly hits in Pakistani evening/night:
    6:30 PM PKT  ← most dangerous time (avoid trading 6-7 PM PKT)
    8:00 PM PKT  ← secondary news
    12:00 AM PKT ← FOMC (very late night)

London session PKT times:
    1:00 PM - 9:00 PM PKT  ← best session for USD pairs

New York session PKT times:
    6:00 PM - 2:00 AM PKT  ← overlaps London 6-9 PM PKT

London/NY overlap (highest liquidity):
    6:00 PM - 9:00 PM PKT  ← best time to trade
    but avoid 6:30 PM PKT on news days
