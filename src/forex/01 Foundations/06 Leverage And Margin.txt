what is leverage
    Think of it like using a small amount of your own money to control a much larger amount in the market.
    Leverage = Multiplier on your money
        If leverage is 1:10, it means:
        With $100, you can trade like you have $1,000.

    Without leverage:
        $100 investment
        10% profit = $10 gain
    With 1:10 leverage:
        You control $1,000
        10% profit = $100 gain
    👉 Your $100 became $200


    Without leverage:
        You lose $10
    With 1:10 leverage:
        10% loss on $1,000 = $100 loss
    👉 You lose your entire $100


    If the loss reaches your deposit, broker will automatically close your trade (called liquidation or margin call).

    Profit/Loss = Price Change % × Leverage

What is Margin?
    Margin = Your own money that you put to open a leveraged trade.

    It’s basically your security deposit.
    Think of it like this:
        You want to trade $1,000
        You only have $100
        Broker gives you 1:10 leverage

    👉 That $100 is your margin.


Margin = Position size ÷ Leverage = 3,200 ÷ 4,000 = $0.80
Free margin remaining = $200 − $0.80 = $199.20

How leverage affect margin:
0.01 lot with 1:100 leverage
    Position size:
        0.01 × 100,000 = 1,000 units
    Required margin:
        1,000 ÷ 100 = $10

0.01 lot with 1:4000 leverage
    Required margin:
        1,000 ÷ 4000 = $0.25
