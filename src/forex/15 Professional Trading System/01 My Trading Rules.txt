these are non-negotiable rules — breaking any = close platform

BEFORE ANY TRADE:
    run pre-trade checklist (no exceptions)
    check higher timeframe direction first
    identify S/R or supply/demand zone
    confirm candle signal at zone
    calculate lot size using trade planner

RISK RULES:
    max risk per trade: 2% of account
    max trades per day: 2-3 (no more)
    if 2 losses in a row → stop trading that day
    never move stop loss further away
    never add to a losing trade

MINDSET RULES:
    if emotional → do not trade
    if trying to recover a loss → do not trade
    if market not clear → do not trade (cash is a position)
    no trading 30 mins before/after major news

EXECUTION RULES:
    entry only on confirmation candle close
    SL always placed beyond structure (not random)
    TP at next S/R or supply/demand zone
    always use trade planner before entering
